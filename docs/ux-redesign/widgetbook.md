## Why

Area-implement dual-truth shots prove host parity. Widgetbook is for **component isolation** (states, signed-out honesty, multi-hash family chips) without OAuth or live inventory.

## Phase 1 (shipped)

Catalog folders under Widgetbook:

- Meta, Cards, Family (incl. multi-hash Base → one chip), Facets, Scope, Empty
- Group (header + outline rail)
- Detail (instance strip, owned/unowned detail, perk grid, toggles)
- Workspace (grid + 400 detail / grid only)
